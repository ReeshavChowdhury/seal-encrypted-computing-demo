WHAT THIS SHOWS

Encrypted values.
Different kinds of math.

BFV gives exact integer addition and multiplication. CKKS works with approximate real vectors, supporting slot-wise addition, multiplication, and rotations. CKKS multiplication needs relinearization and rescaling; rotations use Galois keys. The demo generates only the rotation key needed for the selected shift.

This is a learning demo, not a secure service for real data. It runs the entire operation in your browser; it does not send inputs or keys to a cloud evaluator. CKKS results are approximate, and the sample parameters are for demonstration only.
