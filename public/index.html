WHAT THIS SHOWS

Numbers stay encrypted
during the operation.

BFV supports exact arithmetic on encrypted integers. Here, the browser creates a fresh key pair, encrypts both values, evaluates their sum or product using the ciphertexts, and decrypts the result with the secret key.

This is a learning demo, not a secure service for real data. It runs the entire operation in your browser; it does not send encrypted inputs to a cloud evaluator. The sample parameters and interface are for demonstration only.
